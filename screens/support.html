Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about using InvestMatch — whether you're a business owner, investor, franchisor, or advisor.

How does the platform ensure profiles are genuine? +
Every profile is manually reviewed by our analysts before going live. We verify email, phone, and social media accounts. Businesses also undergo document verification including GST certificates and registration documents. Profiles are rated out of 10 based on completeness and verification level.
When do contact details get shared? +
Contact information (name, phone, email) is revealed only when both parties express mutual interest and a match is established. This "Available after connect" pattern ensures confidentiality and prevents unsolicited outreach.
What types of transactions are supported? +
We support full business sale, partial stake sale, investment/equity funding, business loans, asset sales, franchise opportunities, and distributorship opportunities.
Is there a fee to use InvestMatch? +
Basic registration is free. Premium subscription plans are available for businesses (NPR 25,500 / 38,500 / 2,55,000), investors (NPR 25,500 / 30,500 / 76,500), and advisors (NPR 1,55,000). All paid plans carry a 1% finder's fee post deal closure. Optional paid services include CIM and valuation reports.
How long does it take to get my business listing approved? +
Profile activation typically takes 2 business days. Our analysts review your business details, financials, and supporting documents before approving the listing.
Can I list my business for both sale and investment? +
Yes, you can select multiple transaction types for your business. You can offer a full sale, partial stake, investment, or loan — all within a single profile.
How do I express interest in a business? +
Once you've created a verified investor profile, you can browse business listings and click "Send Proposal" or "Contact Business" on any listing. The business owner will review your profile and decide whether to connect.
Is there a limit on how many businesses I can contact? +
Free members have a daily limit on connection requests. Premium subscribers get unlimited connection requests and priority visibility.
How do I register as an advisor? +
Select "Advisor / Broker" during registration, or visit the How To page and choose "Register as an Advisor". You'll need to provide your credentials, specializations, and track record.
What information do I need to list a franchise opportunity? +
You'll need your brand details, number of existing franchisees, year established, headquarters location, expansion regions, expected monthly sales, space requirements, and total investment required.
