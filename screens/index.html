Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InvestMatch.

How does the platform ensure profiles are genuine?

Every profile is manually reviewed by our analysts. We verify email, phone, and social accounts. Businesses also undergo document verification (GST, registration certificate, financials).

When do contact details get shared?

Contact info is revealed only when both parties express mutual interest. This prevents unsolicited outreach and protects your identity until you're ready to connect.

What types of transactions are supported?

Full business sale, partial stake sale, investment, business loan, asset sale, franchise, and distributorship opportunities — across all industries and regions.

Is there a fee to use InvestMatch?

Basic registration is free. Premium plans start at NPR 25,500. A 1% finder's fee applies post deal closure on paid plans. No hidden charges.
